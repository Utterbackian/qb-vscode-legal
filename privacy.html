Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 7, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how [Your Name/Company] ("we," "us," or "our") handles information in connection with our VS Code extension that integrates with QuickBooks Online ("the Extension").

1. Information We Access

The Extension uses QuickBooks Online's OAuth 2.0 authorization flow to connect to your QuickBooks company file. With your explicit consent, it may access accounting data you authorize, such as invoices, customers, or transactions, solely to perform the functions you request within the Extension.

2. How We Use Information

Data retrieved from QuickBooks is used only to display and process it within your local VS Code environment as part of the Extension's functionality. We do not use this data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose unrelated to the Extension's stated function.

3. Data Storage and Transmission

The Extension operates locally on your machine. OAuth tokens and any QuickBooks data retrieved are stored locally and are not transmitted to or stored on any third-party server operated by us, except as required to communicate directly with Intuit's QuickBooks Online API.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your QuickBooks data with third parties. Data flows only between your local machine and Intuit's QuickBooks Online API, governed by Intuit's own privacy policy.

5. Data Retention

You may revoke the Extension's access to your QuickBooks account at any time via your Intuit account settings or by disconnecting within the Extension. Disconnecting removes locally stored authorization tokens.

6. Security

OAuth tokens are stored using your operating system's secure credential storage where supported. We take reasonable measures to protect data handled by the Extension but cannot guarantee absolute security of data transmitted over the internet.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
